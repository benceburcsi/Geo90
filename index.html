About Geo90 & Game Rules

Geo90 is an interactive daily geography trivia game designed to test your knowledge of world countries, flags, capitals, and geographical features. Every day at midnight CET, a brand new criteria unlocks globally.

How to Play: You have 90 seconds to type matching country names in English. Each correct answer builds your score, while incorrect guesses deduct 3 penalty seconds from your clock. Play daily to build your streak and compare your percentile rank against players worldwide!
